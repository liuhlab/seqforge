Assay guide: pull-down assays (ChIP-seq, ChIP-exo, CUT&RUN, CUT&Tag)

In a pull-down assay, an antibody enriches the chromatin bound by one protein or one histone mark.
A control library, such as an input or an IgG library, measures the background.

- The target is a label, never a factor. Write each mark or protein the study pulls down as a
  value of target, with its quote. Never list target as a factor, even when the study pulls down
  several marks. Never list IgG, input or "none" as a value of target.
- control_type names background-control libraries only, in the source's words, such as input,
  IgG or a no-antibody library. A mock-treated sample is a baseline, not a control, and a
  spike-in is not a control library. With no control library, control_type has no values, its
  gap is na, and ties stays empty.
- Under ties, list which control goes with which pull-downs. A tie names one control library and
  the pull-down libraries made from the same material, such as one chromatin preparation or one
  batch of cells or nuclei. Quote one unbroken run of words that the same field of the control's
  record and of every one of those pull-downs' records holds, such as "liver rep1" in the titles
  "input liver rep1" and "H3K4me3 liver rep1". When the target splits the shared words, quote the
  piece that carries the replicate, such as "_rep1" in "liver_H3K4me3_rep1" and
  "liver_input_rep1". Otherwise quote a sentence that says this control and these pull-downs came
  from the same material.
- When one control serves pull-downs made from different material, such as a pooled input or one
  IgG for all conditions, tie it only to the pull-downs made from its own material, or to none.
  List a tie only when such words exist. Never pair libraries by a guess.
