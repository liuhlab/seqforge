# 43. A hypothesis steers which candidates are computed, and never what they score

A span-verified chemistry claim looks like evidence and the matrix has a slot waiting for it, but at
any weight above zero there is a byte margin the paperwork overturns — identical FASTQs with
different prose would then yield different `dataset_hash`es, and R9's recorded rung would stop
separating a call the data made from one the paperwork made. A hypothesis therefore has zero
evidential effects and three control-flow ones: it widens the scored pool, it breaks a
processing-divergent tie, and it is the asserted side of conflict detection. One that names the
winner settles such a tie at rung 0 on an `asserted` basis and raises a `resolved` conflict; one
naming only its family keeps the byte rung, because the bytes still chose the leaf.

**Status.** Absorbs ADR-0040 — how a tie the prose broke is recorded.
