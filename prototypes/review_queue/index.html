Review queue: where does a person settle a unit?

Three places a person could work the queue of bounding units, each a group of linked GEO records. Records and their links are real public GEO; the model answers, pass history and queue counts are made up.

What the engine stores

One entry per unit a person answered, from any of the three variants. A person's answer outranks the models and holds until the unit's records change.
