How to read this page

Each study below is a group of public records that should be read as one piece of work. Its bounds say which GEO Series belong to it and which papers describe it.

A small model wrote a draft of the bounds from the GEO records and the paper abstract. A larger model, the judge, then checked each draft and could read anything, the full paper included. Where the judge disagreed, its version is the one in use. Those studies come first, then the ones the judge was not sure about.

You are the third reader. Follow the links, then mark the study: Accept if the bounds in use are right, Fix to tick which Series and papers really belong, Drop if the study should leave the set. A note helps whoever reads your answer later. Nothing waits for you, so read as many or as few as you like.

Your marks stay in this browser. When you stop, press Export answers: it saves one file, which one command then files beside the studies.

Keys: J next study, K previous, A accept, F fix, D drop, N write a note, U clear the mark, Esc leave a text box.
